What makes a project rise?

EARLY SCORE combines contract verification, holder base, holder concentration, market-cap/volume signals when available, and early-stage characteristics. A higher score means stronger detected signals — it is not a buy recommendation and cannot guarantee safety or performance.

01 · Discovery

Projects are pulled automatically from Robinhood Chain's Blockscout token index.

02 · Evidence

The engine reads verification, holders, supply, token type and concentration data when available.

03 · Ranking

Projects are scored and filtered into Early, Verified and other views without manual curation.
