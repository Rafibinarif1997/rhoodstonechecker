STAKE YOUR STONE.

Lock your Rhood Stone on Robinhood Chain. One staked Stone makes your wallet OG eligible. Minimum stake is 7 days, followed by a 7-day unstake cooldown.

YOUR STONES

Select a Stone you own and approve the staking contract. Approval is a one-time NFT permission; staking itself is a separate transaction.

Connect wallet to load your Stones.

STAKED STONES

Your active stakes are read from the staking contract. During the 7-day cooldown, the Stone remains staked and OG status remains active.

Connect wallet to load your stakes.

HOW IT WORKS

01
APPROVE
Allow the staking contract to transfer the selected Stone.
02
STAKE
Stake your Stone. The 7-day minimum starts immediately.
03
UNSTAKE REQUEST
After 7 days, request unstake. A 7-day cooldown begins.
04
COMPLETE
After cooldown, complete unstake and receive your Stone back.
