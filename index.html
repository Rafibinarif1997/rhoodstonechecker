THE IDEA

Not just an NFT.

Rhoodstone is a community-driven NFT experience on Robinhood Chain. The stone is the symbol. The holder is the story.

“Hold the stone. Keep your place. Build what comes next.”

Rhoodstone is designed around a simple holder-first experience. Connect your wallet once, prove ownership on-chain, and unlock the private holder experience. The ecosystem can grow in phases without making the core experience complicated.

FAQ

Simple by design.

What network is Rhoodstone on?

Rhoodstone is built for Robinhood Chain mainnet.

How is holder status verified?

The site checks the Rhoodstone NFT contract balance for the connected wallet on-chain.

Do I connect again inside Holder Area?

No. The homepage stores the connected wallet session and the Holder Area reuses it.
