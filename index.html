THE IDEA

What is Rhoodstone?

Rhoodstone is a community-driven NFT built around a simple idea: holding a stone means holding a place in an ecosystem that grows with its community.

Phase 1 is intentionally simple. Connect your wallet once and verify your Rhoodstone ownership. Future utilities can be introduced in phases while keeping the holder experience simple.

FAQ

Simple by design.

Do I need to connect on every page?

No. The site keeps one wallet session and internal pages do not need another Connect button.

How is holder status checked?

The app calls the Rhoodstone NFT contract balanceOf(wallet) on Robinhood Chain.

What if I disconnect?

The website clears its local wallet session. Connect again to restore access.
